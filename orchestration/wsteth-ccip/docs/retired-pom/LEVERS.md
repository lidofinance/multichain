# Levers — who can pull what, with which role

> **September 15 deployment:** use [Current deployment and POM permissions](CURRENT-DEPLOYMENT.md)
> and the [deployment report](deployment-2026-09-15.md). The detailed POM descriptions below
> are historical: proposal modes, guardian/veto/approval APIs and combined pause roles no longer apply.

**One table, one row per gate.** Every mutating call that changes the system's behaviour, the
actor(s) that can originate it, the role each of them uses, the contract it lives on, and the POM
timelock it has to serve. Levers that share an actor, a role, a timelock and a contract are stacked
into one row, one per line — the row states a single way in, and lists everything it opens. Where two
principals instead reach the same lever through two different checks, both are listed in that lever's
row: actor lines then their timelocks in **Actors and timelock**, contract then the matching role
lines in **Contract and roles**.

This is the lever-first transpose of
[`PERMISSIONS.md` §2.1](./PERMISSIONS.md#21-the-complete-levers-map--every-actor-role-and-lever)
(actor → role → lever). Read that file for *why* the model is shaped this way, `PAUSES.md` for the
pause/recovery asymmetry, [`mcms-levers.md`](./mcms-levers.md) for the one-principal deep dive, and
[`PARAMETERS.md`](./PARAMETERS.md) for the values.

## How to read it

- **Both chains.** Rows hold on L1 and L2 unless a cell names **L1** / **spoke**. `L1-POM` /
  `L2-POM` are two independent `PoolOperationManager` deployments. The one selector that splits
  them is `unpause()` (`0x3f4ba83a`): **Blocked** on the L1 hub, **3 d Veto** on every non-L1
  spoke — so MCMS can queue `hooks.unpause()` on a spoke and restart `BRIDGE`. See
  [`PAUSES.md`](./PAUSES.md) and [`config/README.md`](../config/README.md) § *The hub/spoke row*.
- **"Lido DAO" is one actor with two on-chain bodies:** the Aragon **Agent** on L1, the
  **`OptimismBridgeExecutor`** (OpExec) on L2. Every row naming it holds on either chain, with the
  local body in the local `POM`'s `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`. What it takes to *move* either body — an
  Aragon vote, the DG delays, and on L2 the OP message plus OpExec's own delay — is not in this
  table; see the scope note below.
- **The Lido DAO is left off every lever it shares with someone else.** It holds
  `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` on both POMs, and that reaches everything one way or another: directly through
  `onlyRoleOrAdmin`, through `directCall` on anything the POM owns, and — since `getRoleAdmin` is not
  overridden, so the admin is role-admin of *every* POM role — through granting itself any role it
  lacks. Listing it on each row would say nothing. **Read every row as "this actor, and also the Lido
  DAO."** The rows that *do* name it are the ones where it is the only way in. Two caveats to the
  "any role" shortcut: the self-grant is a separate, observable transaction and is **not** a bypass
  inside one call (`propose` / `cancel` — see [Not levers](#not-levers-and-why)), and the
  **DAO-direct** route it implies carries **no timelock and no veto window**, so the delay in
  **Actors and timelock** binds the named actor, not the DAO.
- **End state.** Holders are the post-hand-over ones — the deployer is revoked everywhere except two
  pinned residuals, of which only one is a row here (CCV `allowlistAdmin`; the other is the OpExec
  cancel-guardian, out of scope below). On the current record the governance principals are single
  EOAs, not the multisigs the rows name (`L-SEP-01`, `PERMISSIONS.md` §4.3).
- **POM governance controls are admin-only.** Current upstream POM has removed `GUARDIAN_ROLE`.
  Lido Emergency stops a malicious MCMS proposal by `shutdownProposalQueue()` — that increments the epoch and
  permanently voids every in-flight proposal. `veto` / `approve`, the five timelock setters, and
  UUPS upgrade authorization are `onlyRole(DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE)`.
- **Two standing routes** reach anything the POM owns (pool · hooks · lockbox · CCV · TAR entry).
  The target's own gate is `onlyOwner` with `owner = L1-POM / L2-POM`, and **Contract and roles**
  names the POM-side role that reaches it. Per the rule above, only the **Queue** route is written out in the
  rows; **DAO-direct** is implicit everywhere except `transferOwnership` (both chains) and hooks
  `unpause()` **on L1**, where it is the *only* route and is named as such. On a spoke, hooks
  `unpause()` also has the Queue route (3 d). The two standing routes:

| Route          | Who                                                     | Role                                                    | Timelock                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **DAO-direct** | Lido DAO → `POM.directCall(target, value, data)`        | `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE`                                    | **none** — immediate, no veto window                      |
| **Queue**      | Chainlink MCMS `propose` → delay → **anyone** `execute` | `PROPOSER_ROLE` to propose; execution is permissionless | the per-lever value in **Actors and timelock**; Emergency `pause()` voids the queue (epoch++) |

- **The timelock in Actors and timelock is the POM timelock only** — the delay `PoolOperationManager` imposes on the
  queue route: **3 d** global (`getGlobalMinDelay`), **14 d** on the six selectors carrying an
  override, `Blocked` on `transferOwnership` (`0xf2fde38b`, both chains) and on `unpause()`
  (`0x3f4ba83a`) **on the L1 hub only**. On a spoke `0x3f4ba83a` falls to the 3 d global. Every proposal also expires **30 d** after creation, so
  the usable execution window is `expiry − delay`. Delays *outside* the POM — Aragon voting, the DG
  timelock, the L2 executor's grace period, OP transport latency — are **not** in this column; see
  [`PARAMETERS.md`](./PARAMETERS.md) §3 and `state/deploy-params-testnet.toml`. `—` means this lever
  serves no POM timelock at all.
- **`onlyRoleOrAdmin(X)`** means the function accepts `X` **or** `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` — the DAO does
  not need a grant of `X` to pull such a lever.
- **Contract and roles** names the deployed contract the function lives on, by its Solidity name,
  then `(L1)` / `(L2)` / `(L1+L2)` for that name's Claim A instance scope, then the role or check that
  gates it; which deployment each contract is, and where it comes from, is in [Contracts](#contracts)
  at the end.
- **Only routes with a live holder are listed.** A role, admin slot or nominee that is unassigned at
  the end state is not a way in, so it gets no line here — the empty ones and what their emptiness
  buys are in [Not levers, and why](#not-levers-and-why).

**What is deliberately absent.** This table stops at the Lido DAO. Its two bodies — the Aragon
`Agent` on L1, `OptimismBridgeExecutor` on L2 — appear only as the **Lido DAO** actor; the machinery
that *drives* them carries no rows here: neither the Aragon apps (`TokenManager` · `Voting` ·
`Agent`), nor the Dual Governance stack (`DualGovernance` · `Escrow` · `EmergencyProtectedTimelock`
and the Reseal / Tiebreaker / Emergency committees), nor `OptimismBridgeExecutor`'s own `queue` /
`execute` / `cancel` and self-administration levers. All of it is Lido-wide infrastructure this
deployment inherits rather than configures, and it is outside the Claim A matrix; it is catalogued in
[`PERMISSIONS.md` §3.8](./PERMISSIONS.md#38-l1-dual-governance--agent-trust-root), and the chain that
actually gets run is in `script/12_resume_lido.sh`.

Two consequences for reading the table. Their delays are **not** in **Actors and timelock**, which is
POM-only — an L2 lever attributed to the Lido DAO still costs an Aragon vote, the DG delays, OP
transport and the L2 executor's own delay before it lands. And on the current testnet record that
upstream path is far weaker than the rows imply: the delays are compressed to minutes
(`afterSubmitDelay` 15 min vs mainnet 3 d, `afterScheduleDelay` 15 min vs 1 d, `voteDuration` 5 min —
`state/deploy-params-testnet.toml`), the deployer holds an LDO voting majority, and every DG
committee is an `ACTORS_MNEMONIC[4..10]` EOA — not a public anvil key, and not a multisig
([`plan-v3-dg-committees.md`](./plan-v3-dg-committees.md)). The older live record still has the
anvil keys.

## Not levers, and why

| Thing | Why it is not in the table |
|---|---|
| hooks `applyAllowListUpdates` | `i_allowlistEnabled == false`, fixed at construction; it reverts `AllowListNotEnabled` for everyone, owner included (`L-NEG-05`) |
| L2 token `bridgeMint` / `bridgeBurn` | the legacy immutable `bridge` authority is absent from the source — removed upstream in the pinned base `lido-l2-with-steth @ feat/token-upgrade` (`L-NEG-07`) |
| POM reversals for a halter | `RESUME_ROLE` is unassigned — a halter cannot undo its own halt **at the POM gate** (`restartProposalQueue` / `unpauseTokenPool` / `unpauseRemoteLanes`); only the admin can. That is not the whole restart story: MCMS still reaches hooks `unpauseRemoteLanes` at 3 d on both chains, and hooks `unpause()` at 3 d on a spoke (`PAUSES.md` §2) |
| POM `EXECUTOR_ROLE` | never granted — that emptiness is what makes `execute` permissionless, so the executor is listed as *anyone*, not as a role |
| pool `rateLimitAdmin` / `feeAdmin` | both pinned `0x0` in `getDynamicConfig` — the pool's two shortcut admins have no holder, so `setRateLimitConfig` / `withdrawFeeTokens` are reachable only through the POM |
| TAR `acceptAdminRole(wstETH)` | `pendingAdministrator` is `0x0` at the end state — no nominee exists to accept, until a `transferAdminRole` creates one |
| Lido DAO `propose` / `cancel` | plain `onlyRole(PROPOSER_ROLE)`, no admin bypass — the DAO must `grantRole(PROPOSER_ROLE, self)` first (an observable tx), or use `directCall` |
| POM acting on its own | neither POM has an autonomous path; every owner lever needs a role-holder's `directCall` or an executed proposal (`D-ACT-08`) |
| CCV `forwardToVerifier` / `verifyMessage` | `view` — gates on the path, not levers (`verifyMessage` additionally carries `_onlyOffRamp`) |
